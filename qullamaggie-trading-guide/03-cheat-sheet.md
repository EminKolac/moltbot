# Qullamaggie Cheat Sheet

### One-page quick reference. Pair with the Playbook (context) and Quant Spec (automation). Educational only.

---

## First, the market (do this before any long)
- **Green light (trade, full size):** index **10-day MA > 20-day, both sloping up**; 100-day not
  rolling over; breakouts/EPs are *holding*.
- **Yellow (cut size / be picky):** one MA starts sloping down; breakouts starting to fail.
- **Red (no new longs; cash / shorts):** 10-day **< 20-day** and sloping down.
- Mantra: *uptrend = "free money"; downtrend = "death by a thousand cuts."* Only ~**20–30% of the
  year** is green. The rest you wait.

## The non-negotiables (all setups)
- **Risk per trade:** **0.25–1%** of account, rarely >1%. Fixed and small.
- **Stop:** structural (**low of day / base low**), **never wider than 1× ADR**. Hard market stop.
- **Size:** `shares = (account × risk%) ÷ (entry − stop)`; cap **~10–25%** per name, **~30% overnight**.
- **Entry trigger (all longs):** break of the **opening-range high** — first **1-min**, then **5-min**,
  then **60-min** candle. Market orders. *"Limit orders are for people who want to miss a good breakout."*
- **Take profits:** sell **1/3–1/2 after 3–5 days or ~2–3R**, move stop to **breakeven**, **trail the
  rest on the 10-day (fast) or 20-day (slow) MA**, exit on a **close below**.
- **Never average down.** Add only on a **brand-new base** (own stop). **No new risk in a red market.**
- Edge = **asymmetry, not accuracy:** ~**25–35% win rate**, small ~1R losers, occasional **10–50R** winners.

## Setup 1 — Breakout / Continuation *(learn first)*
- **Scan:** top **1–2% gainers** over 1/3/6 months (e.g. +30–100%+); **ADR% ≥ 5–6%**; **$ vol > ~$100M/day**;
  tight consolidation **~2 weeks–2 months** "surfing" the 10/20-day, **volume drying up**.
- **Enter:** ORH (1m→5m→60m) of the breakout day; **volume should expand**.
- **Stop:** low of breakout day, ≤ 1× ADR. *Rationale: a real breakout works right away — it shouldn't revisit the LOD.*
- **Manage:** partial at 3–5d / 2–3R → breakeven → trail 10/20-day. Bull-market winners often **10–20×** risk.
- **Best when** the market is **just coming out of a correction** (tricky after a month straight up).

## Setup 2 — Episodic Pivot (EP) *(his current main setup)*
- **Scan:** **gap ≥ +10%** on a real **catalyst** (usually earnings/guidance); **NOT already up** the
  prior 3–6 months (must be a genuine surprise); **huge volume** (≈ avg daily volume in first 15–30 min);
  opens **above the base and the 50-day**. Quality EP = **mid/high or triple-digit EPS+rev growth, big beat**.
- **Enter:** ORH (1m/5m/60m), holding above open/VWAP. **Stop = low of day**, ≤ 1× ADR.
- **Manage:** partial in 3–5d → breakeven → trail **10/20-day** (strong EPs can ride the **20/50-day** for months).
- *"EPs have been 50× better than breakouts"* in choppy years; *"the best setup for most people"* (prep before the open).

## Setup 3 — Parabolic Short *(advanced — years of profitability first)*
- **Scan:** large-cap **+50–100%+** (small-cap **+300–1000%+**) in days/weeks; **3–5+ up days**, accelerating,
  exhaustion (new highs on declining volume, climax bar).
- **Enter (wait for the roll-over):** break of **opening-range low**, or **first red 5-min candle**, or
  **VWAP fail** on a bounce. **Stop = high of day / VWAP reclaim** (tight). **Smaller size** than longs.
- **Cover into the 10/20-day MA** (first bounce zone); trail rest lower. Mind overnight gap risk.
- Reward is smaller: **~5–10×** vs 30–50×+ for longs. *"Don't be too early — let the amateur shorts get run over."*
- **Mirror long:** after a short collapses **50–60%+**, snapback long on ORH / first green 5-min (bounce 50–100%).

## Indicators he actually uses
**10- & 20-day MA** (surf + trail; SMA or EMA, *"doesn't matter"*), **50/100-day** (context/regime),
**ADR%** (stop + size), **volume / RVOL**, **VWAP** (intraday, shorts). Tools: **TC2000**, **Finviz**, eSignal, IBKR/Centerpoint.

## Top mistakes (incl. his own)
Forcing longs in a red market · **overtrading** (his self-described "biggest weakness") · hesitating on
stops · averaging down · chasing extended moves · stops too tight for the stock's ADR · trading choppy /
illiquid names (he prefers **NASDAQ** momentum over NYSE) · buying **biotech ahead of a known data date** ("Russian roulette").

> Most edge is in **risk management + patience + setup mastery** (his Evernote DB: **1000+ hours, 7–8 years,
> thousands of charts**). The EP alone takes **1–2 years** to learn. This is a craft, not a shortcut.
