- "Triple-digit EPS and revenue growth. A stock that's been going nowhere for years. They raised
  revenue guidance from 170 to 190 million, from 128 — that's an insane raise, a huge surprise to the
  market. Enormous volume, broke out of a big base. Over 200% [move]. That's five-star." — *EP party!*
- "Forward earnings is everything. The market is forward-looking — it doesn't care about current
  earnings, it cares about guidance / forward growth." — *Market mechanics*
- "Nvidia gapped up because consensus was 7 billion and they guided 11 billion — 4 billion over what
  analysts expected. How often does that happen?" — *Market mechanics* (illustrates guidance-surprise scale)
- "Sometimes a sector becomes hot AFTER an EP — after the leading stocks in that sector start having
  EPs, that's when it becomes hot." — *EP party!*

## Exits / trailing nuance (important caveat to the 10/20-MA trail)
- "You can't use a 10- or 20-day daily moving average for a stop on these [same-day parabolic movers] —
  that's just stupid. By the time it gets there it gives back all the move. You have to sell into
  strength." — *Speculation money is back!* (re: stocks up 200–300% in days, e.g. VBOS, CCC)
- "'You have to sell into strength' — no you don't. If you bought the EP on INDO and sold the next day
  at +130% you missed big money. For micro-caps I don't believe in selling into strength; better to use
  an intraday moving average — I use the 10 and 20 EMA on the 60-minute." — *EP party!*
  (Reconciles to: scale out into climaxes, but trail the runner on the 60-min 10/20 EMA, not the daily.)
- "They're surfing the 10 EMA — pulled back to the 10 EMA, undercut, reclaimed, built higher lows."
  — *Market mechanics*
- "I switched to the 10 EMA on the daily a year ago... it doesn't matter, you can use the 9 EMA or the
  10. As long as it's good enough. In a trending market these moving averages work like magic — they're
  literally magic lines." — *Market mechanics* (SMA vs EMA is not critical)
